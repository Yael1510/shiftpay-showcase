Chapters

What it does

Clock in three ways

A shared tablet with employee PINs, a phone with an optional location check, or a manager entering a missed shift.

Know who's on duty

A live board shows who is working, on break, late or absent, with alerts by email for lates, no-shows and forgotten clock-outs.

Plan the week

A weekly schedule with days off and swaps. Lates and undertime follow the schedule, overnight shifts included.

Approve, then pay

Review punches in bulk, approve overtime, and run payroll for the 1st–15th or 16th–end cutoff in minutes.

Payslips and reports

Payslips staff open on their phone, the payroll register, a bank payout file and monthly remittance totals.

Start in an afternoon

Paste your staff list from Excel or Google Sheets. A preview catches mistakes before anything is saved.
